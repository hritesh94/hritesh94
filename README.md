### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a roboticist on a mission to make **robotic prosthetics affordable and accessible** for differently-abled people worldwide. I work where hardware meets learning: servos and microcontrollers on one side, continual learning and kinematics on the other.

- 🤖 Designed and built a **6-DOF robotic arm**
- 🧠 Reproduced ML research papers: **GRACE** and **Key-Value Bottleneck**
- 💡 Engineered a **laser-based Li-Fi** communication prototype
- 📡 Researched **OFDM / PAPR reduction** techniques
- 🌱 Currently leveling up in **ROS 2 · Gazebo · SLAM**

📍 Kolkata, India &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🎓 B.Tech ECE, HIT Kolkata (2021–2025)
